## Control-design correction

The fixture originally labeled random is xorshift32, a linear GF(2) state transition. Its bitplanes have degree at most 32 by Cayley–Hamilton, explaining the 87-byte result for 4096 bytes. It is a positive linear-generator control, not a generic random negative control. ASCII, nibble and alternating-high fixtures inherit that linear generator; their low-plane savings cannot be attributed to alphabet restriction alone. Original measurements retained unchanged. E0019 adds deterministic SHA-256 counter-stream controls.
