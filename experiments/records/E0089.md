# E0089 — Bounded full-integer Mersenne GCD

Planned. Exact GCD reconnaissance beyond trial division. Let N=32Q from E0087. For k=720720 and 12252240 (lcm(1..16) and lcm(1..18)), M=2^k-1 is odd; gcd(N,M)=gcd(Q,M)=gcd(N mod M,M). A prime factor p with multiplicative order of 2 dividing k is captured (p-1 dividing k is sufficient but not necessary). This is the fixed-base, fixed-exponent Pollard p-1 identity, not a new algorithm or a general factorization guarantee. Because 2^k is congruent to 1 modulo M, full k-bit blocks can be summed modulo M, with a partial final block handled by ordinary Horner reduction. No full-size integer materialized. A nontrivial GCD may be composite; do not label it prime. Nested bounds may yield overlapping factors; never multiply them blindly.

Primary Rust/GMP calculates streaming residue r and extended GCD d,s,t. Independent Node BigInt uses half-width chunks and shift-and-mask folding, checks identical r, d divides both r and M, d>0 and s*r+t*M=d. These prove d is the exact GCD. Hashes of witness and residue logged; GCD logged in hex (bounded to 4096 bits). Both corpus hashes must match; full verified evidence required before factor ledger update. No new compression claim. Per-case Rust timeout 180 s, independent verifier 240 s and 2 GiB Node heap cap; any failure remains unverified. Standard hosted runner only.

E0088 found full-width modular arithmetic expensive, and its fixed exponent stage-1 timing becomes unrepresentative when the modulus is larger than 2^720720. E0089 directly exploits the small auxiliary modulus; max working modulus 12,252,240 bits, approximately 1.46 MiB. Independent certificate multiplication is bounded by this scale, not corpus size. No novelty claim.
