# C17 — Lucas sequence multiplicative reconnaissance

Active. C16 fixed-base p-1-style searches were negative; evaluate a p+1-style auxiliary modulus, not a larger Mersenne exponent. For V_0=2,V_1=3,V_{n+1}=3V_n-V_{n-1}, roots have product one and discriminant 5. For odd primes p not dividing 5 where 5 is nonsquare mod p, their order divides p+1; therefore p+1 dividing k implies p divides V_k-2. Other primes can also divide it. For even k, V_k-2=5F_k^2. Primary GMP Fibonacci construction and independent Lucas doubling plus Bezout verification. E0091 tests one bound k=720720; no exhaustive coverage, general p+1 algorithm or novelty claim. Prior art: Williams, A p+1 method of factoring (1982), https://doi.org/10.1090/S0025-5718-1982-0658227-7. Require verified proper GCD, account for known powers of 2 before any factor update.
