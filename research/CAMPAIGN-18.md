# C18 — Affine radix subspace descriptions

Active. Investigate exact linear algebra over GF(2) on rows of radix digits, rather than further ordinary integer factor searches. Distinct from C06 global recurrence synthesis and C09 lag differences: an affine basis and packed coordinates express every row in a bounded group, including correlations across bit positions. Constant-bit masks are a matched control so gains cannot be attributed solely to ASCII top bits. Gaussian elimination and affine coordinates are established mathematics; no novelty claim.
